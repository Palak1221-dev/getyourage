Many resumes read like a copy of a job description: *"Responsible for managing the database and helping with deployments."* This tells a recruiter what you were supposed to do, not what you actually achieved.

## Measure Quality, Not Just Years
Modern ATS filters assess the quality and impact of your work by looking for numerical indicators. Resumes that include numbers score significantly higher.

## The Quantification Formula
Transform your bullets from task-oriented to impact-oriented:
* **Task**: *"Helped speed up the website page load times."*
* **Impact**: *"Optimized database queries and asset pipelines, reducing frontend latency by 40% and serving 10M+ monthly concurrent users."*

Always search for:
1. **Revenue impact** (e.g., base sales, savings in dollars, budget management)
2. **Scale metrics** (e.g., codebase size, transaction rates, user volume)
3. **Leadership markers** (e.g., team size, coordination roles)
