A "Ghost Job" is a job description posted by a company that they have no immediate intention of filling. Companies keep these postings live to build a talent pool, project growth, or appease internal staff.

## Why Ghost Jobs Damage Your Search
Applying to jobs takes time. Submitting custom-tailored resumes to ghost jobs wastes your energy on black holes.

## How to Spot Ghost Job Red Flags
1. **Evergreen Wording**: Phrases like *"We are always looking for talent"* or *"Rolling basis applications"* indicate resume farming.
2. **Missing Salary/Location Details**: Fake JDs are often vague, lacking clear compensation metrics or division context.
3. **Copy-Paste Responsibilities**: Look for extremely generic templates that contain clichés like *"work with cross-functional teams"* without specifying team roles.
4. **Draft Placeholders**: Check if JDs have accidental brackets like `[Company Name]` or `[Insert Date]`.
