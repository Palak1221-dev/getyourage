When formatting your resume, you generally choose between two layouts: **Reverse-Chronological** (listing jobs starting with the most recent) and **Functional** (grouping by skill sets, hiding dates).

## The ATS Rejection of Functional Resumes
ATS parsers are designed to map skills to timelines. They need to calculate *how long* you have used a technology. A functional resume lists your skills in one section and your companies/dates in another. Because the parser cannot link the two, it assumes your experience duration is 0 years.

## Stick to Reverse-Chronological Layouts
* **Link Roles to Achievements**: Keep all bullets tied directly to a specific company and date range.
* **Format Dates Clearly**: Use standard formats like `MM/YYYY - MM/YYYY` or `Year - Present`. Custom date formats can cause parsing calculation failures.
