There is a massive amount of misinformation online regarding how Applicant Tracking Systems operate. Let's debunk the top myths so you can focus on what actually moves your score.

## Myth 1: "ATS systems auto-reject resumes"
* **Reality**: ATS systems do not automatically reject resumes based on scores alone. They filter and sort candidates. A human recruiter still looks at the application list, but they start with the highest-ranked candidates first. A low score simply pushes you to the bottom.

## Myth 2: "You must save your resume as a TXT file"
* **Reality**: Modern ATS systems read PDF and DOCX files perfectly. In fact, a PDF is preferred to keep your layout template consistent, provided you avoid using text boxes or columns.

## Myth 3: "Keyword stuffing works"
* **Reality**: Stuffing irrelevant terms in small fonts will get you flagged. Recruiters review the parsed text. If your skills don't align with actual chronological accomplishments, they will reject the profile.
