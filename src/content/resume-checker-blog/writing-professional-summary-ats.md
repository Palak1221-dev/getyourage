A professional summary sits at the top of your resume. When structured correctly, it acts as a high-density keyword zone for ATS indexing and a strong introductory hook for human recruiters.

## Avoid the Generic "Objective Statement"
* **Weak (Objective)**: *"Motivated team-player seeking an entry-level software engineer role to grow my skills."*
* **Strong (Summary)**: *"Full Stack Engineer with 5+ years of experience architecting event-driven web applications using React, Node.js, and AWS. Proven track record of optimizing database performance, reducing API latency by 35%."*

## The 3-Sentence Formula
1. **The Hook**: Your title, years of experience, and core technologies.
2. **The Impact**: A metric showing your most significant career win.
3. **The Target**: What you specialize in and aim to build for their team.
