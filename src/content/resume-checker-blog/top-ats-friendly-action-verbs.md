The first word of every bullet point on your resume is one of the most heavily scrutinized areas by both machine parsers and human eyes. Leading with a passive phrase immediately weakens your authority.

## Passive vs. Active Language
* **Passive**: *"Was responsible for managing the project team."*
* **Active**: *"Spearheaded project coordination, leading a team of 5 engineers to deliver features 2 weeks early."*

## Action Verbs by Category
* **For Leadership & Strategy**: *Spearheaded, Led, Orchestrated, Chaired, Governed, Directed, Steered.*
* **For Tech & Development**: *Architected, Engineered, Developed, Coded, Configured, Automated, Deployed.*
* **For Efficiency & Savings**: *Optimized, Reduced, Consolidated, Streamlined, Trimmed, Maximized.*

Ensure at least 80% of your experience bullets open with a strong action verb to maintain a high scoring tier.
