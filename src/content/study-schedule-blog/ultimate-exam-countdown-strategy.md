Exam season is stressful for every student. But the students who walk into the exam hall feeling calm and prepared share one thing in common: they used a countdown strategy.

This guide outlines a complete exam countdown plan, from 30 days out to the night before your exam.

## Why You Need a Countdown Strategy

Without a timeline, studying becomes reactive. You open a textbook, read whatever feels urgent, and hope it sticks. A countdown strategy flips this: you decide in advance what to study and when.

The [Tooltails Study Scheduler](/study-schedule/) includes an automatic countdown tracker that shows exactly how many days remain for each exam, helping you stay aware of your timeline.

## The 30-Day Exam Countdown

### 30 to 21 Days Out: Survey and Plan

At this stage, your goal is not deep learning. It is understanding the full scope of each exam.

**Action items:**
- List every subject and topic that will be on the exam
- Gather all notes, textbooks, and resources
- Identify your weakest areas
- Set a daily study hour target

Use the [study planner](/study-schedule/) to add your exam date and subjects. The scheduler will calculate how many hours you need per day to cover everything.

### 20 to 11 Days Out: Intensive Learning

This is your core study phase. Each day, cover 2-3 new topics using active learning techniques.

**Study technique for this phase:**
1. Read or watch a lesson on the topic (20 min)
2. Summarize in your own words (10 min)
3. Solve practice problems or answer questions (20 min)
4. Review your summary the next day (5 min)

### 10 to 4 Days Out: Revision and Practice

By now, you should have covered all material. This phase is about reinforcement.

**Focus on:**
- Solving past exam papers under timed conditions
- Reviewing topics where you scored low in practice
- Creating summary sheets for each major topic

### 3 Days to Exam Day: Final Polish

At this point, do not try to learn anything new. Focus on review and confidence.

**Each day:**
- Review your summary sheets
- Skim through difficult topics
- Test yourself on key formulas or definitions
- Get adequate sleep (at least 7-8 hours)

## Daily Countdown Routine

| Days Remaining | Focus | Time Investment |
|---------------|-------|----------------|
| 30-21 | Survey & plan | 2-3 hours/day |
| 20-11 | Core learning | 4-6 hours/day |
| 10-4 | Revision & practice | 4-5 hours/day |
| 3-1 | Final review | 2-3 hours/day |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What if I have less than 30 days?** Condense the phases. Spend 2 days on surveying, then move directly to intensive learning. Prioritize high-weight topics.

**What if I have more than 30 days?** Use the extra time for deeper learning. Spend more days on difficult topics and add extra revision cycles.

**How do I handle multiple exams in the same week?** Shift your schedule so the earlier exam gets priority in the final week. Use the multi-exam support in the study planner to balance study time across exams.
